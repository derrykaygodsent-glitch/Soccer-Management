## Founding-window timing

For each nation, derive the annual club-founding window from its existing league calendar: open 84 days before league-entry applications open, and close 14 days before league-entry applications open. The seasonal window governs only applications to found a new club. It must not gate year-round licensing applications for already registered clubs, and it is separate from league entry and its player-list requirements. Applications already submitted continue to a decision after the founding window closes. When closed, show the next window and useful preparation actions instead of the founding form.

## Real-world grounding for future discipline and appeals

FIFA’s Club Licensing Handbook describes the national association’s club-licensing manager as the main point of contact between the association and applicant clubs, and provides for written licensing decisions and an appeals body. FIFA’s Football Tribunal is a formal dispute forum for parties and matters within its jurisdiction, not a general walk-in club office.
