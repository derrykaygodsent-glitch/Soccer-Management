# Player and tactics programme

1. Position, foot and role: sided familiarity, duties and role movement.
2. Team shape and transitions: paired shapes, triggers and rehearsal.
3. Player intelligence: visible skills, hidden personality and preferred moves.
4. Match readiness and dressing-room relationships: persistent fitness, sharpness, knocks, morale, cohesion, friendships, competition for minutes, settling and manager interventions.
5. Development, registration and career history. This includes the following acceptance criteria, not just profile fields:
   - Separate season and competition lists, filing windows, eligibility and deadlines; national and club cups must not share one global list. Preserve existing FA team-entry rules.
   - Assign unique season/competition shirt numbers, handle requests, returning loanees, transfers and contested numbers. Never silently strip a number from a teammate.
   - Make omissions, late additions, captain/number disputes and shirt changes visible to the affected player, teammates, supporters and press. Responses depend on relationships and personality; let the manager explain a decision.
   - Enforce the filed list and numbers at selection, kickoff, substitutions, simulated fixtures and checkpoint resume. Keep historical lists and number changes alongside minutes, injuries, contracts, honours and development records.
   - Test player progression with age, coaching, facilities, minutes and injury setbacks, and enforce registration rules independently of player potential or reputation.

## Phase 5 delivery order

- **5A · Founder recruitment and preseason:** local unattached players first, personal introductions and repeat approaches, paid scouting stays with saved discoveries, professional ambition, a longer founder calendar and requested, playable preseason friendlies. Keep existing saves on their dates.
- **5B · Competition registration and shirt numbers:** implement the competition-specific lists, eligibility checks, player discussions, supporter reactions and history described above. An FA entry application in 5A is not a substitute for this system.
- **5C · Development and career record:** test and connect coaching, facilities, minutes, injuries, player progression and the historical player dossier.
