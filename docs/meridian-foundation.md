# Meridian Football Universe · Foundation 1.0

`src/meridian.js` is the canonical offline content pack for new careers. A settlement ID includes its country code: Valdoria's Vesterholm and Norvaska's capital are distinct places. Every founding club has an explicit home settlement. The eight derby records link club IDs, not ambiguous city names. Continental identities describe football culture, not attributes imposed on individual players.

## Current field and future structure

The atlas names 100 professional clubs across 100 settlements. The career prototype runs founding-club district standings, not a full national pyramid. The design calls for independent national associations, promotion and relegation between three 16-club divisions, and national, league and super cups plus reserve, U21 and U18 leagues. This gives 60 divisions, eventually 960 clubs. Do not invent the other 860 just to fill slots.

The Europa Champions Cup is intended for 32 entrants from four countries. The other continental competitions have names but no approved field sizes yet. The World Club Championship is intended for 32 clubs across all five continents, the International Nations Cup for national teams every four years, the Global Super Cup for the five continental champions, and the yearly Intercontinental Cup for selected champions. These events are plans, not scheduled matches. The listed rivalries are records, not yet attendance or reputation modifiers.

## Atlas first

Before adding the remaining clubs, approve a map: coastlines, borders, regions, city locations, rivers, mountains, roads and transport links. Then locate new clubs, develop grounds and histories, populate the divisions, define national teams, seed past seasons and finally run ongoing club decisions. No borders or travel distances have been assumed from the ordering of the country list.
